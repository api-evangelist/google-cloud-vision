Change Product Search legacy category migration In 90 days, the legacy categories "apparel" , "homegoods" , and "toys" will be upgraded. You will still be able to use these categories. However, when running searches, they will perform similar to the "apparel-v2" , "homegoods-v2" , and "toys-v2" categories.
