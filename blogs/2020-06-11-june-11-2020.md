Change OCR legacy model access extension Based on customer feedback, we have decided to extend support of the legacy TEXT_DETECTION and DOCUMENT_TEXT_DETECTION models. These legacy models are accessed by specifying "builtin/legacy_20190601" in the model of a Feature object.
